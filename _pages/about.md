## Research

NL2Spec: translating natural-language requirements into formal specifications, and from there into provably correct (verified) program code.

One line of this work, SpecBridge, targets **formal specification synthesis**: it learns a *formalization plan* from natural language and uses it to guide the generation of a formal specification, which makes downstream formal verification possible.

## Research direction

Turning natural-language requirements into formal specifications, and further into provably correct (verified) program code — NL2Spec. The work touches formal specification synthesis, program synthesis and formal verification.

## Ongoing projects

### SpecBridge — learning natural-language formalization plans for formal specification synthesis

* Period: 2026 – present
* Advisors: Yun Lin (Shanghai Jiao Tong University), Zhenjiang Hu (Peking University)
* My role: co-author (first author: Wenjie Zhang)
* Outcome: the paper *SpecBridge: Learning Natural-Language Formalization Plans for the Formal Specification Synthesis Task* was accepted at [NeurIPS 2026](/en/publication/specbridge-neurips-2026)

The project studies **formal specification synthesis**: it learns a *formalization plan* from natural-language requirements and uses it to guide the generation of a formal specification, making downstream formal verification possible.

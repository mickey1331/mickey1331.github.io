## Summary

SpecBridge studies the formal specification synthesis task: given requirements expressed in natural language, it learns a *formalization plan* and uses it to produce a formal specification that downstream formal verification can consume. The work belongs to the broader direction of generating provably correct program code from natural-language requirements (NL2Spec).
